About Our College

The TIPS Career Builders was established in 1998 with a commitment to education and student development.

We aim to provide students with opportunities to build knowledge, confidence and practical skills. Our goal is to prepare students for a successful future.

OUR MISSION

"To transform students into highly successful, innovative, and capable professionals."
